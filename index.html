STEP 2: INFORMED CONSENT

Please read the following carefully.

1 Purpose of Services

I understand that I am seeking psychiatric–mental health services, which may include evaluation, diagnosis, treatment planning, psychotherapy, medication management, and/or other mental health interventions provided by a qualified mental health professional.

2 Nature of Treatment

I understand that psychiatric–mental health treatment may involve:

  • Clinical interviews and mental health assessments
  • Psychotherapy or counseling
  • Prescription and management of psychiatric medications
  • Coordination of care with other healthcare providers
  • Review of my medical, psychological, and social history

The specific treatment approach will be discussed with me and may change based on my needs.

3 Benefits and Risks

I understand that potential benefits of treatment may include improvement in symptoms, functioning, and overall well-being.

⚠️ Risks may include:

  • Emotional discomfort when discussing sensitive topics
  • Possible side effects of medications
  • No guarantee of improvement

4 Alternatives to Treatment

I understand that alternatives may include declining treatment, seeking services from another provider, or using non-psychiatric interventions.

5 Medications (if applicable)

If prescribed, I understand the purpose of the medication, possible benefits and side effects, risks of not taking medication as prescribed, and my responsibility to report side effects or concerns.

STEP 3: CONFIDENTIALITY

Your rights and our privacy obligations.

6 Confidentiality

I understand that information shared during treatment is confidential and protected by law under HIPAA (Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act).

Exceptions to confidentiality include:

  • Risk of harm to myself or others
  • Suspected abuse or neglect
  • Court orders or legal requirements
  • Coordination of care with other healthcare providers (with consent)

7 Client Rights and Responsibilities

I understand my rights include:

  • Ask questions about my treatment at any time
  • Participate actively in treatment decisions
  • Withdraw consent at any time (with notice)
  • Access to my medical records
  • Request amendments to my records
  • Receive notice of privacy practices

My responsibilities include:

  • Provide accurate and complete information
  • Inform provider of any changes in my condition
  • Follow treatment recommendations
  • Inform provider of medications from other sources
