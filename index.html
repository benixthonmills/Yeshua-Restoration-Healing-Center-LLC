STEP 4: CONFIDENTIALITY

6.
Confidentiality

I understand that information shared during treatment is confidential and protected by law under HIPAA (Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act).

Exceptions to confidentiality include:

  • Risk of harm to myself or others
  • Suspected abuse or neglect
  • Court orders or legal requirements
  • Coordination of care with other healthcare providers (with consent)
7.
Client Rights and Responsibilities

I understand my rights include:

  • Ask questions about my treatment at any time
  • Participate actively in treatment decisions
  • Withdraw consent at any time (with notice)
  • Access to my medical records
  • Request amendments to my records
  • Receive notice of privacy practices

My responsibilities include:

  • Provide accurate and complete information
  • Inform provider of any changes in my condition
  • Follow treatment recommendations
  • Inform provider of medications from other sources
